How to play

Goal. Fill every cell with consecutive numbers, from 1 to the last cell, so that each number is adjacent (including diagonally) to the next.

Ink-marked cells are givens — fixed clues you can't change.

Drag from any filled number across empty neighboring cells to extend the chain automatically. Drag back a step to retract.

Tap an empty cell to enter a specific number by hand.

Red highlights mean two touching numbers aren't consecutive, or a number's used twice. Fix those to finish.
